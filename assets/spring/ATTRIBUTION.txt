Changes for this real-time learning story: baked geometry and pose, reduced
subdivision, mobile PBR material conversion, texture downsampling, and an
articulated head group. Hair uses the authored preview geometry with procedural
surface detail, not the full offline particle hair. This is a licensed fictional
character, not a likeness of Luoyi or any other real child.

The companion concept preview used a Spring turnaround by Julien Kaspar,
Blender Studio, CC BY 4.0. That preview is not included in the public game.
